With an investment from Chinese smartphone maker Xiaomi Corp, three former Foxconn executives have established an audio device company called 1More Inc. to challenge Apple Inc.’s audio unit Beats Electronics LLC in the global market. After selling over 10 million earphones in China in 2014, the two-year-old startup is now looking to expand into the U.S.
